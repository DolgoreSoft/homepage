Menubeam lets store owners edit menus, prices, sold-out status, and notices in a phone app and shows the changes on a store TV menu board. This policy covers the Menubeam app and the Menubeam TV app. If the Korean and English versions differ, the Korean version prevails.

1. Information we collect

CategoryDataWhen
AccountEmail, password (for email sign-up, handled by the authentication service), account identifier and email provided by Google or Apple sign-inSign-up and sign-in
Store contentStore name, menu names, prices, and descriptions, menu photos, logo, background images, notices, slides, TV screen names and settingsWhen the owner enters or uploads them
TV deviceAnonymous account identifier of the TV app, pairing code, linked store and screen, last-seen timeWhile the TV app runs or is paired

We do not collect location or contacts and we do not show ads. The camera is used only to take menu photos and scan the TV pairing QR code, and the photo library is accessed only for photos you choose. QR scanning video is not sent to our servers.

2. How we use it

  • Sign-in and account management
  • Storing menu board content and showing it on TVs
  • Pairing phones and TVs and showing TV online status
  • Applying plans, answering inquiries, and fixing problems

3. Retention and deletion

  • Account and store content: Kept until you delete your account, or longer if a law requires it.
  • TV pairing data: Deleted when you unpair the TV or delete your account.
  • TV anonymous account: A device identifier with no name or email. It remains after an owner account is deleted so the TV app can be paired again. Clearing the TV app's data ends that device's link to it.
  • Pairing codes: Expire after about 9 minutes and can no longer be used.

Deleted data cannot be recovered. See the account deletion page for how to delete your account.

5. Your rights

You may request access, correction, deletion, or suspension of processing of your personal information. You can edit or delete store content in the app and delete your account with "계정 삭제" (Delete account) in the app. For other requests, email the contact above.

6. Children

Menubeam is for store operators and is not directed to children under 14.

7. Security

We use encryption in transit and access rules so that only the owner of a store and its paired TVs can access its data.
