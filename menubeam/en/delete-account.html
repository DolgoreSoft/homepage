Menubeam Account Deletion

Effective date: November 2, 2026
Operator: Dolgoresoft
Contact: main@dolgoresoft.com
한국어: 한국어 버전

Delete in the app

  1. Sign in to the Menubeam app.
  2. Tap the account icon at the top right and choose 계정 삭제 (Delete account).
  3. Confirm your identity and confirm the deletion.

Request without the app

If you removed the app, email main@dolgoresoft.com from your sign-up email with the subject "Menubeam account deletion request". We process requests within 7 days after confirming your identity.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in account
  • Store information, menus, notices, slides, and TV screen settings
  • Menu photos, logo, and background images
  • TV pairing data (paired TVs return to the pairing screen)

What remains

  • The TV app's anonymous device identifier (no name or email, and no longer linked to a store)
  • Information we must keep by law, for the required period

Deleted data cannot be recovered.
